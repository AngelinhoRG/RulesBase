## Card Actions

### Draw Four Wild Card

This card allows you to call the next color played, before the next player takes their turn. However, that player must draw 4 cards from the DRAW pile and add them to their hand and then forfeit their turn. BUT, the player that puts this card down must not have any other color cards that can be played instead of this card, if it is found that they did, and a challenge is made by the player forced to draw the cards, they must reveal their hand to the challenger. If the challenge is successful, the original player must draw the four cards instead. If the challenge is unsuccessful, the challenging player must draw the four cards, plus two additional cards, for a total of six cards.

There is no official rule allowing a player to "stack" a Draw Four (or Draw Two) on top of another Draw Four or Draw Two to pass the draw penalty to the next player. The player who draws the cards from a Draw Four must draw them and forfeit their turn; stacking is a house rule variant only and is not part of the official rules.

### Draw Two Card

When a player plays a Draw Two card, the next player in sequence must draw two cards from the DRAW pile and forfeit their turn. As with the Draw Four, there is no official rule permitting the next player to play their own Draw Two in response to avoid drawing cards.
