## Law 12: Fouls and Misconduct

### Sending-Off Offences

A player, substitute or substituted player who commits any of the following offences is sent off with a red card:

1. Serious foul play.
2. Violent conduct.
3. Spitting at an opponent or any other person.
4. Denying an opponent or the opposing team an obvious goal-scoring opportunity by deliberately handling the ball (this does not apply to a goalkeeper within their own penalty area).
5. Denying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity to an opponent by an offence punishable by a free kick or penalty kick.
6. Using offensive, insulting or abusive language and/or gestures.
7. Receiving a second caution (yellow card) in the same match.

A player, substitute or substituted player who has been sent off must leave the vicinity of the field of play and the technical area. The player's team may not replace them with another player; the team must continue the match with one fewer player for the remainder of the match.

### Caution (Yellow Card) Offences

A player is cautioned and shown a yellow card if they commit any of the following offences: unsporting behavior, dissent by word or action, persistent infringement of the Laws of the Game, delaying the restart of play, failing to respect the required distance when play is restarted with a corner kick, free kick or throw-in, entering or re-entering the field of play without the referee's permission, taking off their shirt at any point while they are on the field, or deliberately leaving the field of play without the referee's permission.
